## a place for the little details

The repos hold the code. [The blog](/blog) is where I explain why something is there, how the pieces fit, and what still needs work.

The [Ship of Harkinian Vita project](/blog/oot-vita-modding) is my attempt to bring desktop-style mod support to the older Vita port. The goal is to use models and textures from packs like [Djipi's 3DS Experience](https://gamebanana.com/mods/477979). The article covers the working converted baseline and the original-archive loading work still in progress.

Find the rest on [GitHub](https://github.com/unkindypie), or [email me](mailto:maya1.bondarenko@gmail.com).
