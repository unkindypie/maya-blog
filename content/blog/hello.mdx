Hi. I'm Maya. I make things for my own setup, then spend a while making them usable for someone else too.

Two projects keep most of those changes: [maya-nvim](https://github.com/unkindypie/maya-nvim), my Neovim config, and [.maya-dotfiles](https://github.com/unkindypie/.maya-dotfiles), my Linux desktop and terminal setup.

The READMEs tell you how to install them. Here I want to talk about the smaller decisions: why personal settings live outside the editor repo, how Light and Dark reach the different apps, and what context a local model actually gets.

A few places to start:

- [maya-nvim: my editor, with room for yours](/blog/maya-nvim)
- [.maya-dotfiles: paper colors, one theme switch](/blog/maya-dotfiles)
- [Local AI in mvim: give the model some context](/blog/local-ai-in-mvim)

Expect configs, Lua, and things I didn't want to debug twice. Probably handheld stuff later too.
